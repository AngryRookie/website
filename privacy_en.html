5. Advertising and Analytics

Many of our Games are free to play and are supported by advertising. This section explains how advertising works in our Games, what data is involved, and the choices available to you.

5.1 What our advertising partners collect

We work with third-party advertising networks, mediation platforms and measurement providers. To select, deliver and measure advertising, these partners and we may collect and process:

  • your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) or another device or app identifier;
  • your IP address, from which a coarse region may be derived;
  • device and technical information, such as device model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • advertising data, such as which advertisements were shown to you, when, whether they were viewed or clicked, and whether an install or in-app event followed; and
  • inferred interests or audience segments, where personalised advertising is permitted and you have consented.

We do not share your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any content you create with advertising partners, and we do not allow our advertising partners to build advertising profiles of users we know to be children.

5.2 Personalised and non-personalised advertising

Where you have given the consent required by applicable law, advertising partners may use your advertising identifier and the data described above to show you advertising that is more relevant to you (personalised or interest-based advertising). Where you have not consented, or where you have opted out, we request non-personalised or contextual advertising, which is selected based on the context of the Game rather than on a profile of you. Non-personalised advertising may still involve the collection of an advertising identifier for frequency capping, aggregate reporting and fraud prevention, where permitted by law.

5.3 Your choices and how to opt out

  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency): on iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your Advertising ID and, where available, opt out of personalised advertising in Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device manufacturer).
  • In-game consent: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we present a consent dialogue before we use your information for personalised advertising or store information on your device for advertising purposes. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time from the privacy or settings option in the Game.
  • Industry opt-out tools: you can also limit interest-based advertising from participating companies through the Digital Advertising Alliance, the Network Advertising Initiative or, in the EU, Your Online Choices.
  • Do Not Sell or Share: California residents can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. See Sections 7.5 and 16.2.

Please note that opting out of personalised advertising does not mean you will stop seeing advertising in our free-to-play Games; it means the advertising you see will be less relevant to you.

5.4 Analytics and attribution

We use analytics, attribution and crash-reporting tools to understand installs, player behaviour, retention, errors and campaign performance. These tools may collect device and app identifiers, IP address, usage events and diagnostic data. We use them to operate and improve our Games, to detect fraud, and to measure whether our marketing is effective. Where required by law, we obtain your consent before using non-essential analytics technologies.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website or application. They typically contain an identifier and some information, such as a site or app name and a few characters. They help us remember information about your activity, such as session state, language preference and other settings, so that your next visit is easier and more useful.

Where our Games include web-based content, and on our websites, we and our partners use cookies, local storage, software development kits (SDKs), pixels and similar technologies to:

  • keep you signed in and remember your session and preferences;
  • remember your language and display settings;
  • measure traffic, usage and the performance of the Games and our campaigns;
  • detect and prevent fraud and abuse; and
  • where permitted and subject to your consent, deliver and measure advertising.

We do not use cookies or similar technologies for purposes other than those described in this Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings, and reset or restrict your advertising identifier in your device settings. Where required by law, we will present a consent notice before setting non-essential cookies, and you may change your choices at any time. For general information about cookies, please visit www.aboutcookies.org. For instructions on changing your browser settings, please refer to the official website of your browser (for example Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge or Opera).

Some browsers and mobile operating systems offer a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" (GPC) signal. Where required by applicable law, we honour GPC signals as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
